# Archetype × Measurement Maturity — A Bidimensional Refinement of the Weber Taxonomy

Weber's four-pattern AI-startup taxonomy classifies firms by *what they sell*. [[weber-taxonomy-2026-gaps]] identifies seven dimensions the 2021 model fails to encode for 2026. An eighth dimension emerges from function-level evidence: **within a single archetype, outcomes distribute bimodally along a measurement-maturity axis the taxonomy does not see**. The operative classifier in 2026 is archetype × measurement maturity, not archetype alone.

## What it is

EY's June 2025 report on FP&A AI deployments ([[ey-fpa-ai-transformation-2025]]) documents a clean bimodal pattern *inside one function*: top 20% of FP&A teams exceed 20% AI ROI; the median around 10%; "fewer than half of FP&A teams can quantify AI ROI" at all. The same archetype-by-Weber's-definition produces three populations with materially different operating outcomes, separated by measurement maturity rather than by what they sell.

The implication for [[ai-startup-business-archetypes-weber]] is structural. Two firms classified as [[ai-charged-product-service-provider]] (Weber Pattern 1) with identical model sourcing, identical [[distribution-moat]], and identical orchestration depth can sit at opposite ends of the EY bimodal: one captures the surplus and instruments it, the other captures the same surplus but cannot prove or sustain it. From a value-capture-at-exit perspective the two firms are unequal even though the taxonomy classifies them identically.

The remedy is bidimensional. Add measurement maturity as an axis perpendicular to archetype. The resulting 4×n matrix maps each Weber archetype against [[measurement-as-causal-upstream]]'s five layers, distinguishing for example *"AI-charged P/S provider, layer-3+ instrumented"* (the durable sub-population) from *"AI-charged P/S provider, layer-0 / ad-hoc"* (the population that will appear in next year's abandonment statistic).

## Evidence

- EY 2025: bimodal FP&A outcome distribution 20% / 50% / 30% within a single function ([[ey-fpa-ai-transformation-2025]]).
- MIT NANDA 2025: monitoring sophistication correlates with deployment success across functions ([[mit-nanda-state-of-ai-business-2025]]).
- Polimi 2025: 46% of large Italian GenAI-users do not measure — the unmeasured tail of the bidimensional matrix ([[osservatorio-polimi-ai-italia-2025]]).

## Tensions

Extends [[weber-taxonomy-2026-gaps]] rather than contradicting it. Where the gaps page identifies seven missing categorical dimensions, this concept proposes a single continuous dimension (measurement maturity) that interacts with all of them. If accepted, the gaps page should split between *missing categories* (model sourcing, GTM motion) and *missing operational axes* (measurement maturity); the seven-versus-eight count becomes a typological question rather than a counting one.

## Open questions

- Does the bimodal distribution in FP&A generalise to [[ai-data-analytics-provider]] (Weber Pattern 3, the largest cluster), and at what concentration?
- Is measurement maturity a state or a trajectory — can a firm at layer 0 reach layer 3 within a finance year, or are the layers structurally accreted over multi-year cycles?
