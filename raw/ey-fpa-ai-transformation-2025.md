## Why this matters to the research

Fills the **single largest functional gap** the wiki has on enterprise team workflows: financial planning and analysis (FP&A) inside large and mid-market enterprises. Current wiki coverage is heavy on ER&I (BMW, SBB, Aker BP, Merck via [[eri-use-case-deltas]]) and lighter on knowledge-work shared-services. EY supplies a function-specific maturity model, measured payback windows, and named distribution of outcomes across adopter cohorts.

Most operationally relevant for the contributor's sustained-ROI lens:

- **Payback under six months for top-cohort FP&A deployments** (vs the industry-aggregate 2–4 years), confirming legal-tech is not unique in beating the five-month threshold — FP&A is the second functional pocket where above-threshold evidence concentrates.
- **Bimodal outcome distribution:** *"fewer than half of FP&A teams can quantify AI ROI, median returns around 10%, only top-performing 20% exceed 20%"* — direct empirical evidence for the bimodal interpretation of [[scaling-gap]] / [[H2_u-curve-of-value]] bottom-right (most miss, a small tail beats).
- **Concrete time-savings:** forecast cycle 28→8 days (retail CFO case), forecast cycle time -30%, statement production -40%, scenario turnaround -70%.
- **Direct cost savings $300–600K/year per 10-person finance team** — quantifies the ticket size of a credible sustained-ROI FP&A deployment, useful for [[H4_rl-specialization-value-pocket]]'s "break-even contract size" open question.
