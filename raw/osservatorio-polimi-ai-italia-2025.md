# Osservatorio Artificial Intelligence — Politecnico di Milano, AI in Italia 2025

**Author(s):** Osservatorio Artificial Intelligence, School of Management, Politecnico di Milano
**Year:** 2025 (presentation cycle: late 2025, public release Feb 2026)
**Venue:** Osservatori Digital Innovation, Politecnico di Milano. The Osservatorio Artificial Intelligence is the canonical longitudinal Italian-market AI research instrument (annual report since 2017).
**URL:** Press release with headline figures: https://www.osservatori.net/comunicato/artificial-intelligence/intelligenza-artificiale-italia/ — full report behind paywall / Osservatori-membership access.
**Primary or secondary:** Primary research (annual survey of Italian firms by Polimi); the public press release is secondary-summary of the primary instrument.

## Why this matters to the research

Closes the **most glaring geographic gap** in the wiki's SME-side evidence. The wiki currently leans on [[oecd-sme-ai-adoption-2025]] for the Italian datum (Italy = G7-widest 4.7× large-small adoption ratio) and on the single Brescia bag-manufacturer case in [[sme-optimiser-cross-functional-stack]] for case-level texture. Polimi's Osservatorio is the **Italian-primary instrument the OECD G7 paper draws on**, and offers a 2025 update with critical new figures:

- **Italian AI market: €1.8 billion in 2025, +50% year-on-year.** 46% from GenAI / hybrid, 54% Machine Learning.
- **71% of large Italian firms** launched at least one AI project in 2025 — but "only 1 in 5 large firms shows good AI pervasiveness across business functions."
- **SMEs: 15% medium / 7% small** — confirms the structural firm-size split the OECD paper documents at G7 scale.
- **Only 54% of large Italian firms using GenAI attempt to measure benefits.** This is the load-bearing methodological datum for the contributor's sustained-ROI lens: most Italian deployments are *not measured*, which means the absence of sustained-ROI evidence is structural, not just a sampling artifact.
- Sector breakdown: Manufacturing + GDO/Retail growing fastest; mature sectors (Energy & Utilities, Telecom & Media, Banking, Insurance) decelerating; PA = 19% of market, SMEs = 18% of market.
