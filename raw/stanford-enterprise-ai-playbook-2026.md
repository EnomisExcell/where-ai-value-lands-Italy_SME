## Why this matters to the research

The **positive counterweight** to the MIT NANDA 95%-failure datum. Stanford documents 51 deployments where AI did produce measurable enterprise value — the structural inverse of NANDA's null sample. Brynjolfsson is the canonical academic on AI productivity and authors the J-curve literature already invoked in [[ai-productivity-firm-level]]. This source plausibly contains the **specific patterns and durations** under which deployments survive past the J-curve trough into sustained-positive territory.

For the contributor's research frame, this is the source that can answer "which sub-population beats the 95% failure prediction, and through what configuration of complementary capital." For [[H2_u-curve-of-value]] it pressures the top-of-U claim with positive case-study density that may or may not generalize to Italian SMEs and EU enterprise team-workflow buyers. For [[enterprise-adoption-ladder]] it likely provides phase-3-to-4 transition mechanics absent from the WEF synthesis.
